# 1. Get a free GitHub Account

If you already have a GitHub account, you can skip this step.

Go to [GitHub](https://github.com) and sign up for a free account.
You may want to use an email address other than your school email as the primary address so that you can keep your account easily after you graduate.
You will associate your `nwmissouri` email account in a later step.

# 2. Configure your name and email address(es) in GitHub

Go to your GitHub Profile (https://github.com/settings/profile if you are logged in, otherwise go to the settings menu in the upper right drop down on the website).
If you have not provided your name in the Name field, do so. 
This makes it easier to for your account to be identified.
You can also fill in any information in your Bio you want, as well as a URL to an external website (if you have a digital portfolio, for example, this would be an appropriate place to mention it).
Note that anything you put here will be publicly visible by everyone (including employers) so keep it appropriate.

If you change any information on your Public profile, click the Update profile button (under Location) to save the information.

## 2.5 Configuring your Northwest Email Account

If you did not use your Northwest email address when setting up your account, go to the Emails settings page (on the left pane, or go to https://github.com/settings/emails).
Add your Northwest email account where the page says "Add email address".  

Having the appropriate email configured is important; GitHub Desktop will automatically associate any work you do with your Northwest email account (because your student laptops use your S number as your username).
This will ensure that your work is correctly associated with you.

# 2.75 Optional Cool Things

The free account on GitHub comes with quite a few features, including both public and private repositories.
For anything you need to do in this class (or hopefully any others) the free account will suffice.
HOWEVER: as a student you can sign up for a free educational pack at https://education.github.com/pack to get access to some neat tools.
You can do this later and attach the education pack to your account.
