---
title: Joining the Northwest GitHub Organization
category: github
---

To view the resources hosted on the `nwmostate` GitHub Organization,  you need to be a member of the organization.  
You will only need to do this once during your time at Northwest.  
You will only be able to access these repositories while your Northwest credentials are valid (as you will need to log in using the Northwest single sign on to view the resources).

1. Visit https://nwmostate-org-invite.herokuapp.com
1. Enter your GitHub username in the box, and click "Add me to organization"
1. You will be told to "Check your EMAIL"; however you can just go to https://github.com/nwmostate
1. You should see a page saying you have been invited to join the organization; click the big blue button
1. Click the "Authenticate to join" button on the next page, then log in with your NOrthwest Missouri State University credientials

At this point you should be able to see the repositories hosted on the organization!
